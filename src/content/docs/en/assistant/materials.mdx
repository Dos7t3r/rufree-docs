Besides syllabi you can upload other course materials and then ask questions about
them.

## Formats and size

The same as a syllabus: PDF, DOCX, DOC, XLSX, TXT, MD, CSV, up to 8 MB per file.

## Who can see them

<Aside type="caution">
Uploads are private to you by default. If you choose to share one, classmates in the
same course can see it — so check first that the file has nothing personal in it,
such as an assignment with your name and student number on it.
</Aside>

## Storage limit

The library has an overall cap. Uploads are refused with a message once you approach
it, and you will need to delete something first.

## Deleting

Materials can be deleted at any time. Deleting something you had shared also removes
it for your classmates.
