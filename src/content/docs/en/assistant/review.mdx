This is the most important step in the whole Assistant flow.

<Aside type="caution" title="Your calendar is never changed automatically">
Everything Assistant reads from a syllabus is a **draft**. Only what you confirm is
written to your calendar.
</Aside>

## What a draft contains

Each item has a type, a title, a time, and **the part of the syllabus it came from**.
That source is there for you to check against — when something looks off, read the
original first.

Four types:

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Exam | Midterms, finals |
| Assignment | Assignments and projects |
| Due date | A deadline |
| Requirement | A course requirement with no specific date, kept for reference |

## What to check

Three things, in order of how often they are wrong:

1. **The date** — especially items the syllabus wrote as "Week 8" rather than a date
2. **The course** — that it attached to the right one
3. **The time zone** — syllabi rarely state one

## After you confirm

Confirmed items enter your calendar as exams, assignments or due dates. From then on
they behave like manual events and can be edited or deleted.

## Items you do not confirm

They stay out of your calendar. Leave them, or delete them.
