With a course feed subscribed, RUFREE checks new posts for exam times and rooms and
updates the matching exam in your calendar.

<Aside type="caution">
This is a **convenience**, not a replacement for your school's official exam
schedule. Treat the official time and room as authoritative.
</Aside>

## What it needs

For an automatic update you need both:

1. an exam already in your syllabus for that course (see [Uploading a syllabus](/en/assistant/syllabus/))
2. a course feed subscribed for that course

Times found in posts are matched against syllabus exams; no match, no update.

## What each outcome means

After each check RUFREE lists what it did with every post.

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Time announced; updated | Found a time you did not have, and wrote it in |
| Time changed; updated and notified | Differs from what you had; updated and notified you |
| Room or length updated | Same time, corrected or added room or duration |
| Matches what you have | The post agrees with your existing time |
| You undid this one; not reused | You reverted this before, so it is not applied again |
| Older than the one in use; ignored | A newer post already took effect |

### When it skips

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| For another section | The post is about a different section |
| No matching syllabus exam | Nothing to attach it to |
| AI was unsure | Confidence too low to risk writing |
| Quote not found in the post | Could not verify it against the original text |
| No exact time | The post mentions the exam but gives no time |
| Date outside the term | Falls outside the term range |
| Implausible end time | The duration does not make sense |
| Section unclear | Could not tell which section it refers to |

## Undoing an update

Any applied result can be undone. The exam reverts to its previous time and **that
post will not be applied again**.

## Surname for exam seating

Some courses split exam rooms by surname. You can store one (up to 60 characters) and
RUFREE uses it to work out which room is yours. Leaving it empty affects nothing else.
