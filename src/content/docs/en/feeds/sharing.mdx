Usually one person per course needs to subscribe for everyone to benefit. Sharing is
**optional and off by default**.

## What gets shared

With sharing on, **classmates who have the same course** can see the posts and exam
times that subscription reads.

<Aside type="caution" title="What is not shared">
What is shared is **the content that was read**, not your Quercus link. Nobody gets
your feed address, and nobody gets into your Quercus.
</Aside>

## Whether your name shows

There are two independent switches:

1. **Share with classmates** — whether the content is shared at all
2. **Show my name to friends** — whether the shared content is attributed to you

The second only means anything when the first is on. Turn it off and classmates see
the posts without knowing who supplied them.

## Feeds shared with you

If you have not subscribed to a course but a classmate has shared theirs, you see
their posts under that course, along with whether the source is in your section, how
many posts there are, and when it last updated.

## You can stop at any time

Change the switches whenever you like. Once off, classmates stop seeing new content
from your subscription.
