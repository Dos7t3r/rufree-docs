Once subscribed, RUFREE checks the course's Quercus Announcements regularly and tries
to pull exam times and rooms out of them and into your calendar.

## What you need first

The course has to be in your **active timetable**. Without an imported timetable, or
without that course in it, you cannot subscribe.

## Getting the link

<Steps>

1. **Open the course's Announcements page in Quercus.**

2. **Find and copy the RSS / feed link on that page.**

   It looks like this:

   ```
   https://q.utoronto.ca/feeds/announcements/enrollment_XXXXXXXX.atom
   ```

   <Shot
     id="feeds-subscribe-01"
     caption="Where the RSS link sits on the Quercus Announcements page"
     alt="The RSS subscription link on a Quercus course announcements page"
   />

3. **Paste it into the course in RUFREE and preview first.**

   The preview reads Quercus once and reports the course code, section, term, how many
   posts there are and how many look exam-related. It exists so you can confirm you
   pasted **the right course**.

   <Shot
     id="feeds-subscribe-02"
     caption="The preview shown before saving"
     alt="RUFREE showing a preview of a Quercus feed link"
   />

4. **Save once it looks right.**

   RUFREE starts reading posts and looking for exam details immediately.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="This link is personal">
A Quercus feed link carries your own token — **anyone who has it can read that
course's announcements**. Keep it out of public places, including public bug reports.
</Aside>

## When the link is rejected

RUFREE only accepts the Quercus announcements format above. The usual mistakes:

- copying the course home page address instead of the feed
- copying the calendar feed (`.ics`), which is a different thing
- a truncated link

## Rate limit

Up to 8 previews or saves per minute per person. Repeated attempts are held off
briefly; wait a moment and try again.

## Refreshing and removing

RUFREE checks on its own schedule, and you can refresh by hand.

Removing a subscription warns you that **exam times it set go back to the syllabus or
ACORN time.**
