  <Card title="Start with your timetable" icon="calendar">
    Almost everything else depends on it. Export an `.ics` file from your school's
    schedule system and import it; versions are kept per term.
    See [Importing your timetable](/en/timetable/import/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subscribe to course feeds" icon="rss">
    Paste a course's Quercus Announcements RSS link and RUFREE reads the posts,
    looking for exam times and rooms.
    See [Subscribing to a course feed](/en/feeds/subscribe/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Know who sees what" icon="setting">
    Every event has its own visibility, and your account has a second set of switches
    on top. Read [Visibility settings](/en/privacy/visibility/) before you share.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Assistant never edits your calendar" icon="approve-check">
    A syllabus produces a **draft**. Nothing reaches your calendar until you approve
    it item by item. See [Reviewing drafts](/en/assistant/review/).
  </Card>

## What this documentation covers

How each feature works and what information it exposes. It does not cover
implementation details, algorithms, keys, or internal configuration.

RUFREE is an independent project, not an official University of Toronto service.
