## Finding people

Search needs **at least 2 characters** before it runs. Everyone's profile lives at
`/people/<username>`.

<Aside type="note">
Only people with "discoverable" switched on appear in search. It is on by default —
turn it off to stay hidden. See [Visibility settings](/en/privacy/visibility/).
</Aside>

## Sending a request

<Steps>

1. **Send a request by email or username.**

   Either works, and a leading `@` on a username is stripped automatically.

2. **Wait for them to accept.**

   Friendship is **mutual**. They get a friend request notification, and until they
   accept you are not friends and neither of you can see the other's schedule.

3. **They accept or decline.**

</Steps>

## What friends can see

Two layers decide this together:

1. **Each event's visibility** — only events marked "Friends" show details to friends
2. **Account-level switches** — whether course details, location and live status show at all

They combine as AND: an event marked "Friends" still shows only as busy if you have
turned course details off.

## Shared free time

Once you are friends you can see the free time you have in common. This uses only the
busy/free layer, so **it still works when every event is set to "Busy" with no details.**

The exception is "Private": those events do not count as busy, so others may read that
time as free.

## Managing a friend

Each friend has these settings. They are **yours alone — the other person cannot see them**:

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Note | A private name for this person, visible only to you |
| Pin | Keeps the people you check often at the top |
| Birthday | Record their birthday, optionally with a reminder |
| Watch schedule | Notifies you when their timetable changes |

<Aside type="caution">
Notes and birthdays are your own records. They are never synced to the other person
and never change their profile.
</Aside>

## Blocking and removing

- **Block** cuts off contact and can be undone later
- **Remove** ends the friendship

Either way they stop seeing anything you marked "Friends".
