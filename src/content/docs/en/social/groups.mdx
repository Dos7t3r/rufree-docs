Groups are for sharing free time with a fixed set of people over time — a project
team, a club, a friend group.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including transferring ownership and deleting the group |
| Admin | Invite people, handle join requests, manage members |
| Member | See the free time shared in the group |

Ownership can be transferred to someone else.

## Two ways to add people

### Direct invitation

An owner or admin can invite an existing user. The invitation goes to that person and
**only takes effect when they accept** — nobody is added without agreeing.

Invitations expire, and a pending one can be cancelled.

### Invite link

Generate a link to send out. Whoever opens it sees a preview of the group — its name
and members' avatars and display names — and decides whether to join.

<Aside type="caution">
Anyone holding an invite link can use it. Think about where you are sending it; if it
goes astray, remove the person and generate a new link.
</Aside>

## What joining exposes

Group members can see events you marked with "Groups" visibility. Events marked
Private, Busy or Friends do not become visible because you joined.

<Aside type="note">
Joining a group is not handing over your timetable. Each event's visibility still
decides.
</Aside>

## Leaving and deleting

- A member can leave at any time
- The owner can delete the whole group

Once you leave, your schedule is no longer visible to that group.
