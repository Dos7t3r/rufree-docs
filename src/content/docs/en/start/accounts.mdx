## Usernames

Your username is your public handle; your profile lives at `/people/<username>`.

The rules: **3 to 24 characters, lowercase letters, digits and underscores only**
(`a-z`, `0-9`, `_`). Usernames are unique, so saving fails if one is already taken.

## Signing in

The web app supports email code sign-in. The Windows, macOS and Android apps and the
web app **share one account**, so your timetable, friends and settings follow you
without setting anything up again on each device.

## What is in your profile

Display name, username, avatar, calendar colour, campus, major, year of study,
birthday, bio and a status line.

<Aside type="note">
These are not all public. Who sees what is governed by the switches in
[Visibility settings](/en/privacy/visibility/) — including one that controls whether
you can be found by search at all.
</Aside>

## First run

The first time you sign in there is a short setup step (username, campus and so on).
Until it is finished, opening settings takes you back to it.
