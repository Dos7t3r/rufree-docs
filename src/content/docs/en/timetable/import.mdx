Your timetable is the foundation. Shared free time, course feeds and syllabus
analysis all depend on it.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Export an `.ics` file from your school's schedule system.**

   <Shot
     id="timetable-import-01"
     src={timetableImport01Img}
     caption="Where to export .ics in the school's schedule system"
     alt="The calendar export button in the school's schedule system"
   />

2. **Open the Timetable page in RUFREE, pick the term, and upload the file.**

   Picking the right term matters: RUFREE keeps only the meetings that **fall inside
   that term's date range**. If nothing matches you get "no meetings from this term
   were found", which almost always means the wrong term was selected.

3. **Review the differences.**

   If the term already has a timetable, RUFREE shows you what is added, changed or
   gone before anything is saved.

   <Shot
     id="timetable-import-02"
     caption="The difference between the new file and your current timetable"
     alt="RUFREE showing the difference between an imported timetable and the current one"
   />

4. **Save, and choose whether to activate it.**

   If you do not activate it, the version is archived and you can switch to it later.

</Steps>

## Two things to check afterwards

<Aside type="caution">
- **Time zone.** Your account defaults to `America/Toronto`. If every class is off by
  the same few hours, fix the time zone in settings rather than editing classes.
- **Repeats and exceptions.** Recurring classes and exceptions such as cancellations
  are expanded into individual meetings. Spot-check a few weeks, especially around
  Reading Week.
</Aside>

## How much fits in one import

A single version holds at most 5000 events. A normal term is nowhere near that, so
hitting the limit usually means the export covered every term instead of one.

## Events you add by hand

Manual events are stored separately from imported classes. **Re-importing your
timetable does not touch them**, and they do not change when you switch versions.
